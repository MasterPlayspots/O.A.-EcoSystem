M0: community and review online. M1: contracts, mock and document checks. M2: small
single-host/HTTP runtime path. M3: accept PS5 and further device profiles separately.
M4: independent reproduction and an explicitly approved alpha. GPU/PS4/MoE/simulation later and separately.

An open hardware gate blocks the respective hardware claim, not the public plan review.
After each completed task, only upgrade capabilities that are concretely evidenced.
